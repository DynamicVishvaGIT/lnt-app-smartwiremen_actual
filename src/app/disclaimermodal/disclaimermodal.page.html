As per Section 194 R of the Finance Act, 2022, TDS will be applicable on benefits or perquisites if value of such benefits and perquisites exceeds Rs. 20,000/- in a financial year.


The rate of TDS will be

  • 10% if PAN No. is available and is not in Specified Category - Please provide copy of PAN Card
  • 20% if PAN No. falls under specified category - Please provide copy of PAN Card
  • 20% if PAN card is not available
